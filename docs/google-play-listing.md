## English

- **App name:** SharkTune
- **Short description:** Offline precision guitar tuner with song tunings, cents, and peg guidance.

### Full description

Start tuning immediately in E Standard. SharkTune puts a large cent reading,
neutral target, measured Hz, target Hz, and the next adjustment direction on
one precise live screen.

Search songs and artists locally to apply their tuning, choose presets for low
tunings and extended strings, and configure your instrument to see physical
peg-direction guidance from the front of the headstock.

Use tension and gauge tools plus tuning-stability observations to look after
your strings. SharkTune works without accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or server
connections. Microphone audio is processed temporarily on your device and is
never recorded or sent away.
